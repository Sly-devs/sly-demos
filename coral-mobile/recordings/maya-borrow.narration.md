# Coral × Compass — Narration

Edit the spoken text under each `## Beat N — …` heading.

- One paragraph per beat. Newlines inside a beat collapse to a single
  space when the script reads it.
- Do **not** rename headings or change beat order: the script reads
  them in order (1 → 7) and feeds them into the 7 narration beats.
- Optional `**Chips:**` block under a beat = lower-third punch phrases
  burned onto the video, evenly slotted across the beat. Keep each
  chip ≤ 6 words, ALL-CAPS, short. Slate/terminal/proof beats skip
  chips when the hero content is already on screen.

---

## Beat 1 — Framing (slate)

Meet Maya. Her savings live in Coral — a wallet where an A.I. agent
grows her money on DeFi. The agent executes through Compass, and every
move it makes is governed by Sly.

## Beat 2 — Coral savings

Right now, Maya has eight dollars earning yield in Aave. She asks her
agent for a small loan against it — without selling.

**Chips:**
- $8 EARNING IN AAVE
- BORROW WITHOUT SELLING

## Beat 3 — Coral borrow, Sly denies

But borrowing takes on real risk. So Sly stops the agent — it cannot
touch Maya's collateral until a human says yes.

**Chips:**
- BORROWING ADDS RISK
- SLY GATES THE COLLATERAL
- HUMAN MUST SAY YES

## Beat 4 — Sly: pending to approve

Over on Sly, the request hits the approval queue — a one-shot
compass-credit grant, pending a human. One click, and it's approved:
single-use, scoped to just this borrow.

**Chips:**
- PENDING IN THE QUEUE
- ONE-SHOT COMPASS:CREDIT
- APPROVED · SINGLE-USE

## Beat 5 — Compass CLI executes

Now the agent borrows through Compass — for real, on-chain. The
C.L.I. executes against Aave, signs with Maya's wallet, and her
position takes on the debt.

## Beat 6 — BaseScan proof

No mock, no shortcut — the transaction is confirmed on Base, signed by
Maya's own wallet.

## Beat 7 — Coral settled

Back in Coral, the loan's done. And Sly holds the whole trail —
requested, approved by Maya, used once, then locked. Every agent move:
gated, signed, auditable.

**Chips:**
- LOAN SETTLED IN CORAL
- REQUESTED · APPROVED · USED ONCE
- GATED · SIGNED · AUDITABLE
