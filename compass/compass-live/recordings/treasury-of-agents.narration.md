## Beat 1 — Framing (slate)

One treasury agent, many junior sub-agents. The treasury can move the
money. The juniors have to ask. Sly enforces both.

**Chips:**
- TREASURY · SUB-AGENTS
- ASK · APPROVE · ACT

## Beat 2 — Watch the hierarchy work

The treasury borrows a dollar — it has the scope already, no human in
the loop. A junior sub-agent tries the same call — Sly denies, but
points to a path: ask the treasury. The junior fires a delegation
request. The treasury approves: half a dollar, thirty minutes, one
shot. The junior retries — and Sly approves, with the audit trail
showing the grant was issued by the treasury, capped under the
treasury's own ceiling.

**Chips:**
- TREASURY SPENDS DIRECTLY
- JUNIOR ASKS PARENT
- DELEGATION ISSUED
- CEILING NEVER RISES

## Beat 3 — Why it matters

You can give an agent a treasury without giving every sub-agent a
treasury. The capability flows down, scoped at each hop, audited at
every step. One human approves once; the juniors operate within the
budget — autonomously, until the cap.

**Chips:**
- CAPABILITY FLOWS DOWN
- AUDITED AT EVERY HOP
- ONE APPROVAL · MANY ACTIONS

## Beat 4 — Outro (slate)

Treasury of agents. Hierarchical delegation, governed by Sly.

**Chips:**
