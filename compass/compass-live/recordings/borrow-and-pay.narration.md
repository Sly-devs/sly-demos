## Beat 1 — Framing (slate)

An agent borrows against the user's crypto savings and lands the cash
in a wallet it can spend. Both moves gated by Sly. Both tracked by
Compass.

**Chips:**
- BORROW AGAINST CRYPTO
- SPEND WITHIN POLICY

## Beat 2 — Borrow then move

Step one: half a dollar of credit drawn against Aave collateral. The
compass-credit step-up grant is active. Sly approves. Compass returns
the signable payload. Step two: that same half dollar moves out of the
Compass-managed safe and into the agent's own account. A second policy
check, a second receipt — and now the agent holds spendable funds.

**Chips:**
- $0.50 BORROWED
- SAFE → AGENT EOA
- TWO RECEIPTS, ONE FLOW

## Beat 3 — Why it matters

You don't have to give the agent a credit card. You don't have to fund
it ahead of time. Crypto-backed credit, drawn and routed under your
policy, ready for the next call. And every dollar of it is auditable
on both Sly and Compass.

**Chips:**
- NO PREFUND
- NO CARD
- AUDIT ON BOTH SIDES

**Chips:**
